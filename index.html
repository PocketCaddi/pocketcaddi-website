How it works

Three steps. The third one is hitting it.

  1. Get to your ball

    PocketCaddi already knows which course you're on, where the pin is, and what the weather's doing.

  2. Look at your phone or wrist

    No menus, no typing in the wind speed. One number for how it plays, one club to hit.

  3. Hit the club

    If it goes wrong, at least it wasn't the club selection. Small comfort, but comfort.

What it accounts for

All the things you'd guess at, measured.

Wind
Relative to where you're facingNot "north-westerly". Into, helping, or off the left. Adjusted for strength.
Temperature
Cold air, shorter shotsA November morning and a July afternoon are different golf courses.
Altitude
Thin air, longer shotsBuilt in Switzerland, where the course might be 1,500 m up the mountain.
Elevation
Uphill and downhillThe height difference between you and the green, from course data.

Your yardages, not a tour pro'sRecommendations are built on what you actually hit, not what the box says.
Course
Pin distance, automaticallyLocation and course data do the measuring so you don't pace it out.

Apple Watch

Leave your phone in the bag.

The Watch app shows the club, the plays-like distance and a wind dial that turns as you do, so "into the wind" always means into the wind from where you're standing.

  • Wind dial uses your heading, not a compass bearing
  • Your iPhone does the heavy lifting and sends results to your wrist
  • Glance, hit, walk

Pricing will be announced before launch. Early access is free.
